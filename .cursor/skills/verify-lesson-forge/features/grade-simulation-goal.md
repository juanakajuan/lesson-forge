# Grade a simulation goal

A simulation with a goal does not finish when the sliders reach the goal. The learner moves the sliders, then chooses `Check`. On the slope-intercept lesson, the goal simulation `make-slope-two` asks for two points whose slope is 2. A correct first Check before the hint gives `Solid` for its objective in the end summary. A miss gives `Review`. `Hint` appears after one miss. `Show answer` appears after two misses.

## Sub-features

- `goal-not-met-at-start` shows the slope `0.50` at the start. Check with unmoved sliders shows `Move a slider first.` and does not count as a miss.
- `goal-solid-first-try` shows the success text after a correct first Check, reveals the numeric question, and gives `Solid` for `slope-from-points` in the summary.
- `goal-review-after-hint` shows `Hint` after one miss. A correct Check after the hint reveals the next block and gives `Review` for `slope-from-points`.
- `goal-reveal-after-two-misses` shows no `Show answer` after one miss and shows `Show answer` after the second miss.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the rendered slope-intercept lesson.
- Choose `Continue` on the opening explanation and on the line simulation.
- Choose `(0, −2)`, then `Check` on the intercept question.
- Choose `Continue` on the slope explanation.
- Choose `Next step (1/4)`, `Next step (2/4)`, `Next step (3/4)`, then `Continue` on the worked example.
- The goal simulation is now on the page. Move the `y₂` slider, then choose `Check`.
- To see the summary, finish the lesson: answer the numeric question, step through the second worked example, do the second goal simulation, do the ordering question, and choose `Finish` on the recap.

## Gotchas

- The sliders on `make-slope-two` are `x₁`, `y₁`, `x₂`, and `y₂` with Unicode subscripts. The start values are 0, 0, 2, 1. `y₂` = 4 gives slope 2. `y₂` = 3 and `y₂` = 2 are misses.
- A Check with the same slider values as the last Check is not graded. It shows `Move a slider first.` and does not count as a miss. Move a slider to a new value before each Check.
- The first simulation also has sliders named `slope m` and `intercept b`. Use `--last` on the second goal simulation, or the fill fails with `expected one slider`.
- `Solid` needs every assessment of the objective to be correct on the first try. `slope-from-points` also covers the numeric question. Answer `-2` on the first Check, or the summary shows `Review` for a reason that is not the goal.
- The ordering question is shuffled on each load. **To the summary** answers it wrong two times, then uses `Show answer`. That makes `line-through-points` show `Review`. That row is not part of this feature.
- The summary is only visible at the end of the lesson. Mastery is not in the page until you choose `Finish`.
- The summary screenshot scrolls past the lesson title. It shows `How it went`. The snapshot holds `heading "Lines: slope and intercept"` as the title proof.
- A reload starts a new attempt on every block. Do not continue a scenario after a different scenario on the same load.
- The second goal simulation, `hit-two-points`, uses the same Check logic. This recipe only grades `make-slope-two`. Do not report `hit-two-points` as verified from this path.
